Getting started

Hoya is Heliolink's courier dispatch and last-mile delivery platform. There are three ways in: the staff sign-in (dispatchers/admins run the operation from here), the driver sign-in (PIN-based, for the mobile-friendly driver app), and the customer portal (for customers to book orders, track them, and view invoices). Which menu you see depends on your role.

{% if role in ['admin','dispatcher'] %}

Adding employees, customers and orders on their behalf

Dispatchers and admins can create orders for any customer directly — go to Operations → New Order, or open Customers → Customer List and click Place order next to a customer to jump straight into the order form with them pre-selected. This is how staff take a phone/email order and enter it on the customer's behalf, separate from the customer placing their own order via the portal.

Creating & managing orders

New Order (Operations → New Order) captures a delivery or collection job: addresses, contact details, pallets/parcels, weight, scheduling (collection/delivery date, same-day/48h/booked timing) and notes. On save, Hoya automatically generates a barcode label for every pallet and parcel, and produces a quoted price using the rate engine (or the customer's flat rate, if set).

The Dispatch Board (Operations → Dispatch Board) is a kanban-style view of every order grouped by status: Pending → Assigned → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered/Failed. Click any order to open its detail page, where you can assign a driver/vehicle, change status, add surcharges, print labels, and see the full event history and proof of delivery once completed.

Route planner

Select a driver and tick the unassigned orders to include, then Build & Optimise Route. Stops are automatically sequenced using nearest-neighbour routing over UK postcode areas — no mapping API key required. You can re-optimise an existing driver's active stops at any time from the same page.

Pending work control board

A live board of every active job, switchable between "by status" (Awaiting Collection / Awaiting Delivery) and "by driver/van". Rows show an SLA tag — amber if within 4 hours of the delivery deadline, red if overdue. Use the inline Driver/Van dropdowns to allocate manually, or click Suggest to have Hoya pick the smallest available van that fits the load plus a free driver automatically.

Fleet & resources

Fleet (Fleet → Drivers & Vehicles) is where you add drivers (each gets a PIN for the driver app) and vehicles (panel van / 2.5t / 7.5t, each with a default capacity and MPG). Resources shows the same list with a focus on live availability status — toggle a driver or van between Available/On Route/Off (Road) as things change through the day; only "Available" resources are offered by the pending board's Suggest button.

Customers & rate settings

Each customer is either flat-rate (a fixed £ per pallet you set directly) or standard (priced by the cost-based rate engine, less an optional discount %). Rate Settings holds the cost parameters (fuel price, driver £/hour, average speed, handling time, fixed cost, margin %, same-day uplift) with a worked example. Regions & Zones lets you set a cost multiplier per UK postcode area group (e.g. Scotland costs more to serve than the Midlands). Rate Calculator gives an interactive what-if breakdown for any postcode/pallet count/vehicle/timing combination.

Invoicing

Billing → Invoices lists every invoice raised. Click Run weekly invoicing to generate one invoice per customer covering every completed (delivered/collected) order not yet invoiced — each line shows the route, pallets, weight, price and a link to that order's proof of delivery. Fuel surcharge % and VAT % (set in Rate Settings) are applied automatically. Mark invoices Draft → Sent → Paid as they progress.

Insights / KPI dashboard

Period-filtered (7/30/90 days) view of jobs handled, pallets/parcels, delivery vs collection split, success rate, and a DIFOT-style (delivered-in-full-on-time, no exception) percentage, plus a per-driver breakdown. Export the underlying data as CSV at any time.

Barcode scanning

Every pallet/parcel gets a unique barcode (printable via "Print Labels" on an order). The standalone Scanner page (Operations → Scanner) looks up any barcode by camera or manual entry. On the driver app, each stop has a built-in scan gate: a stop can't be marked complete until every box on that order has been scanned, unless the driver explicitly overrides with "complete with items missing" (which flags the missing items on the order).

Run sheets & bulk label printing

Operations → Print Run Sheet generates a printable, driver-and-date-specific manifest — every stop for that driver on that day, with a barcode per stop, a COD column, and blank space to note anything by hand. This is the paper backup for when a phone/tablet isn't practical.

Operations → Bulk Print Labels picks a date and prints every pallet/parcel barcode label for every order scheduled (collection or delivery) on that date in one go — the same idea as exporting shipping labels for a day's jobs, rather than printing one order's labels at a time.

Employees

Admins only: Employees in the top nav lists every staff (dispatcher/admin) account. Add a new one with a name, email, password and role, deactivate an account instantly (it can't be used to log in again until reactivated), or reset someone's password. You can't deactivate your own account. This is separate from Fleet → Drivers & Vehicles, which manages the delivery drivers themselves (PIN login to the driver app, not email/password staff accounts).

Cash on delivery & contactless signing

Set a cash on delivery amount when creating an order (dispatcher form or customer portal) to flag that payment is due on arrival. The driver app shows a "collected £X" checkbox on that stop, and it's tracked on the order and visible on run sheets.

When an order is marked In Transit, Hoya automatically texts the customer a link to a contactless signing page — they can confirm delivery, sign, and confirm COD collection from their own phone, instead of signing the driver's device. You can also find this link on the order's detail page at any time. If the recipient reports a problem instead of confirming, that's logged as a delivery exception the same way a driver-reported one would be.

Note: driver location on the order page is a one-off "last known position" ping taken when the driver opens a stop, not continuous live GPS tracking on a map.

Using the driver app

Sign in with your name and PIN. My Route shows your stops in optimised order, with the next stop highlighted. Opening a stop shows the address, contact, and a scan gate — scan every box (camera or type the code manually) before you can complete the stop, unless you tick the override checkbox. Use the Google Maps / Waze buttons to navigate. To close a stop, enter the recipient's name, a typed signature, optionally take a photo, add notes, then Confirm & Complete Stop — this automatically advances you to the next stop on your route. If something goes wrong (no answer, refused, damaged), fill in the exception reason instead and the stop is logged as failed with the reason recorded.

Using the customer portal

My Shipments lists your orders with live status. New Order lets you book a delivery or request a collection/pickup — fill in the addresses, pallets/parcels and timing, and Hoya handles the rest. Click Track on any order to see its full status history and, once complete, its proof of delivery (recipient name, signature, photo). Invoices lists everything billed to you, with each line itemised back to the shipment and a link to its proof of delivery.

FAQ & troubleshooting

An order won't complete on the driver app. Check the scan progress count on the stop page — if it's short of the total, either scan the remaining boxes or tick "complete with items missing".

A price looks wrong. Check the customer's pricing mode (Customers page) — flat-rate customers ignore Rate Settings entirely and use their fixed £/pallet instead.

SMS notifications aren't sending. Without Twilio credentials configured, Hoya logs SMS messages instead of sending them (visible in the server logs) — this is expected in local/dev use.

Need something else? See the README in the project for architecture and deployment details.

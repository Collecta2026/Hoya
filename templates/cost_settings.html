How a job's miles are worked out

Depot to the furthest stop and back, using the road miles in your rate card's postcode schedule (e.g. SK13 = 42 mi each way, so 84 mi). Wavelength jobs use the round-trip miles typed on the booking. You can change the miles on any quote or job if the real run is different. This assumes the van does the job on its own, so if it shares the run with other drops, the real cost per job is lower.
